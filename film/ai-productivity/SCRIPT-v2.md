## Rules for the picture

- **Every click** shows a pressed state and a changed state after it.
- **Words on screen are real but short.** Names, dates and numbers are samples, never a real client.
- **Claude** appears as a raspberry square with a sparkle, not Anthropic's logo.
- **Slack** is drawn in the film's colours, with no Slack logo.

## Facts checked (2026-11-06)

- **Slack:** Claude in Slack answers in the thread or DM where it is tagged ([Anthropic help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12461605-using-claude-in-slack)).
- **Gmail, Google Drive and Microsoft 365:** Claude's connectors read and search them ([Claude connectors](https://www.claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started)).
- **Shared documents:** Projects hold shared documents for a team ([Projects](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9517075-claude-projects)).
- **Sending email:** Claude does not send email itself, which matches scene 9, where the person clicks Send. The guideline that stops the email is a rule we set up with the organization.
