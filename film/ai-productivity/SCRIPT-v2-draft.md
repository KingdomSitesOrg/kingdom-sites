# AI productivity film: script v2 (draft for Thomas, 2026-11-06)

What changed from v1:

- Claude is introduced by name and job ("Meet Claude, an AI assistant").
- Every feature names a place people know: Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, a spreadsheet.
- The approval line now includes the school's guideline. Send is pressed first and is stopped; Approve, then Send.
- The draft is about 165 words. At the current voice pace that comes to about 80 seconds, still under the 90-second cap.

Facts checked on 2026-11-06:

- **Slack:** Claude in Slack answers in the thread or DM where it is tagged ([Anthropic help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12461605-using-claude-in-slack)).
- **Gmail, Google Drive and Microsoft 365:** Claude's connectors read and search them ([Claude connectors](https://www.claude.com/docs/connectors/getting-started)).
- **Shared handbook:** Projects hold shared documents for a team ([Projects](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9517075-claude-projects)).
- **Sending email:** Claude does not send email by itself. The approval guideline in scene 9 is a step we set up for the school.

All names, dates and numbers on screen are samples: Hillside School, Maria Reyes, March 15, and so on. Claude appears as a raspberry square with a sparkle, not Anthropic's logo. The Slack window is drawn in the film's colours and has no Slack logo.
