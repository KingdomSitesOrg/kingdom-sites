## Thomas and Kingdom Sites

- Kingdom Sites is the studio of Thomas Klein, a software engineer. He designs, builds, ships and maintains websites, iPhone and Android apps, and the software behind them, and he offers AI consultation.
- It is one engineer, on purpose: when someone writes in, they talk to the person who builds the work. Thomas takes on a small number of projects at a time and would rather stay with a handful of products for years than ship something and disappear.
- He works for businesses, churches and anyone who needs software.
- The studio is based in Rochester, Minnesota. Thomas and his wife Monisha are currently in the Philippines at IGSL, the International Graduate School of Leadership, training in biblical studies and discipleship. Client work carries on from there on the same timelines.
- The site's line: "Made to be used. Made to be yours." Every screen shown on the site is from software Thomas built or worked on.
- Thomas is taking on new projects now, and welcomes anyone with an idea to get in touch.

## Background

- Thomas is a software engineer who works across the whole of a product: design, the web and phone apps, the backend and databases, payments and sign-in, and putting it live on the App Store, Google Play and the web.
- His own products include Farzana Classroom, a Latin learning game, Tap to Tick and KCUPG (below). He also works as a contractor on the team behind Ruta, a live service-management platform for landscaping businesses.

## What he makes

- **Websites**: a site that says what you do, shows up when people search for it, and that you can keep up to date without calling anyone.
- **iPhone and Android apps**: designed, built and put on the App Store and Google Play under the client's name, then kept working through every new phone and system update.
- **The software behind them**: sign-in, payments, class lists, schedules, a dashboard for the office. The parts that make a site or an app actually run the work.
- **AI consultation**: helping people use AI well: where it fits in their work, setting it up, and building it into the tools they already use. Hands-on sessions for developers, admins and anyone exploring AI past the chat box: loops, goals, connectors, skills and instruction (.md) files. He also builds AI into apps themselves (answers from a business's own data, actions with a person in the loop). Sessions are booked from the AI consultation page (/ai-tooling).
- He usually builds with Next.js, React and TypeScript for the web, Expo (React Native) for iPhone and Android from one codebase, and Supabase for databases and sign-in.

## The work

### Farzana Classroom (a platform Thomas designed and built)
- Education software for schools, training organizations and education businesses: one proven platform, with each school's own features built on top. Each school gets its own site, name and crest, with its classes, families and payments in one place.
- Families find a class, try a free sample, sign up and pay online at any hour; the teacher sees who is enrolled, who was active this week and who just joined.
- What Thomas made: the design, the web app, sign-in, payments and the teacher's desk.
- Runs on the web, phones and desktop. Status: in beta, first school coming soon. Website: farzana.app. The screens on this site show Wren Hall, a made-up demo school.
- "Farzana" means intelligent, or learned, in Persian.

### A Latin learning game (designed and built by Thomas)
- A Roman-legion Latin practice game for iPhone and Android, for homeschool and classical students. Students march a legion north across Italy, thirteen stops from Rōma to Gallia; every city teaches one piece of the classical curriculum. Nine ways to drill the grammar (declensions, verb endings, real Latin sentences), case challenges, and a leaderboard to race each other on.
- What Thomas made: the design, the iPhone and Android apps, the levels and the leaderboard.
- On the App Store and Google Play. More at /latin-game.

### Ruta (contract work)
- Ruta is service-management software for landscaping and maintenance businesses: an office app, a phone app for the crews, and a portal for customers. Thomas works on it as a contractor on Ruta's team. The product is Ruta's; he did not start the company.
- Areas he has worked on: recurring billing and customer payment arrangements; the crew app (visits, the time clock, working without a signal); office tools for the day's schedule, messaging and reviewing finished work; customer self-service; the shared backend; and the AI assistant inside the product. Website: getruta.com.

### KCUPG (designed and built by Thomas)
- For local ministry in Kansas City: a dashboard of the city's communities from North Africa to East Asia (people, languages and faiths, and the places to meet them) on one map, so churches and ministries see which communities live where. Live at kcupgs.com.

### Tap to Tick (designed and built by Thomas)
- An iPhone expense tracker: log any purchase in a tap or two, including the cash in your pocket, and see the month by account and category. Everything is free, including a shared ledger for two people over iCloud. On the App Store; more at /tap-to-tick.

### Delta Development Project (website)
- The website for a project of SIM Bangladesh working with river-bank and urban communities in northern Bangladesh and Dhaka: village schools, vocational training and disaster response. Live at deltadevelopmentproject.com.
